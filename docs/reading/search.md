The search box in the header — or **Ctrl/⌘+K** (or `/`) anywhere on the
site — searches the published pages of the language being read and, inside
a versioned project, the version being read.

## Finding things while you type

Results appear as you type, in a small window you can move through with the
arrow keys and open with Enter. **Enter** without picking a result opens the
full results page.

Search matches **word beginnings**: `instal` already finds "Installation".

## Typos

A word that appears nowhere in the published docs is corrected to the
closest one that does — one wrong letter in a short word, two in a longer
one, swapped letters counting as one — and the results page says so:
*Results for* installation, *searched as* instalation.

Only published words are ever suggested: a draft cannot leak through a
"did you mean". The same correction helps the
[documentation chat](/p/docuwaves/pages/the-documentation-chat) find its
sources when a question contains a typo.

## Searches that find nothing

Those are the most useful searches there are: a reader said, in their own
words, what is missing. DocuWaves keeps an anonymous tally of them for the
authors — see [the gaps radar](/p/docuwaves/pages/reader-feedback-and-link-checking#what-readers-searched-for-and-did-not-find).
